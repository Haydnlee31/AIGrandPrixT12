# Download catalog data without approval delays

*Access trials and database imports checked 25 October 2026, Singapore time. All nine source counts are verified against the local database.*

**Mymah Finds can proceed without eBay or another account-approval dependency.** We checked **23 dataset/API candidates** and loaded **263,787 real or historical product records**, **581 Microsoft sample products**, and **20,000 independently generated synthetic products**. The local database now contains **284,368 verified source records**; this is not a claim of globally unique products across sources. Amazon Berkeley Objects and Wayfair WANDS provide the largest complete general catalogs; Open Facts and UCI add category coverage. The synthetic source is necessary for reliable merchant, currency, price, stock and variant scenarios because the real datasets largely lack current offers. This integration supersedes the earlier eBay/affiliate-first route; existing approved feeds can be enabled later without delaying this build.

## Synthetic offers fill a specific gap

The selected real datasets provide **product knowledge**, not reliable current shopping offers. A marketplace domain, manufacturer name or Open Facts `stores` field does not identify a verified merchant selling that variant now. UCI unit prices are historical transactions, and Microsoft's supplier rows describe fictional sample relationships. Those fields should remain source attributes with unknown current merchant, currency/price, shipping and stock where appropriate. This follows the actual source schemas and the publishers' descriptions of their datasets. ([ABO fields](https://amazon-berkeley-objects.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/listings/README.md), [WANDS schema](https://github.com/wayfair/WANDS), [UCI variables](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii), [Microsoft samples](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sql/samples/sql-samples-where-are))

The loaded **20,000-product synthetic source** supplies invented merchants and explicit offer scenarios separately. It gives the application a controlled dataset for exercising merchant, price and stock behavior without attaching invented claims to real branded products. Its source and `data_kind` must remain synthetic in the backend and developer-facing metadata. This does not require the forbidden word “prototype” in the frontend, nor does it expand the current Library/Search/Profile scope. Synthetic examples should be excluded from real-data retrieval-quality scores or reported separately.

WANDS also supplies a stronger next step than adding more unverified records: its **480 queries and 233,448 relevance judgments** can evaluate retrieval against the downloaded 42,994-product corpus. Keep evaluation judgments separate from the user-interest model, and reserve a held-out split before tuning. This is a proposed use of the publisher's relevance set, not a measured accuracy result. ([WANDS dataset and judgments](https://github.com/wayfair/WANDS))

## Preserve source rights and verify retrieval behavior

For ABO, retain its **CC BY 4.0** attribution and indicate normalization changes; its first-party grant covers the dataset assets, including images. The downloaded image-reference table contains **398,212 entries** and the metadata join populated image URLs for **147,127 ABO records**. No photo binaries were downloaded; those URL mappings are not a claim of completed image rendering or remote-image availability testing. WANDS and Microsoft samples require their MIT notices. UCI requires CC BY attribution, using the **Online Retail II** citation when importing II rather than reusing the original Online Retail citation. ([ABO license](https://amazon-berkeley-objects.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt), [ABO image documentation](https://amazon-berkeley-objects.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/images/README.md), [WANDS notice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wayfair/WANDS/main/LICENSE), [Microsoft notice](https://github.com/microsoft/sql-server-samples/blob/master/license.txt), [UCI II](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii))

Open Facts metadata must remain in separately identified source datasets with attribution and an export of the derived database under **ODbL**. Keep private Library/evidence and closed affiliate feeds out of those derived datasets. Open Facts images are excluded from this pass because they have separate attribution/share-alike and graphical-rights considerations. Source separation and an export are implementation measures, not a blanket legal conclusion about every future combined use. ([Official data-license guidance](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-server/blob/main/docs/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side.md), [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/))

The completed import results, source-specific import IDs and elapsed times are recorded in [`local-data/catalog-load-results.json`](../local-data/catalog-load-results.json); independent SQL counts verified the **284,368-record total**. Artifacts, checksums and HTTP trial manifests are under `local-data/catalog-trials/{general,domain,seeds}/`. Retrieval, pagination, source isolation and expiry/revocation checks passed. SQLite full-text indexing is installed; representative local query measurements are summarized below. The existing catalog API framework and Library/Search/Profile interface scope remain unchanged. No paid vendor, account approval or eBay activation was needed to load these catalogs.

## Verified integration status

The database contains 284,368 records across nine sources. SQLite full-text indexing is installed; six representative local ABO/WANDS searches measured 23–335 ms using the documented token-prefix semantics. The portable catalog ZIP contains separate source exports and notices, including ABO builder credits, MIT notices, and Open Facts ODbL/DbCL terms. API and client integration checks passed. The complete portable bundle was restored into a fresh migrated database and reproduced all 284,368 records without private Library data. Library/Search/Profile remain unchanged.
