# Alternatives to chat for Mynah Finds

Research date: 25 October 2026. Mynah Finds collects user-shared reels, posts, articles and screenshots. The opportunity is to turn that collection into something users can manipulate, try or share when they return. A sequence of summaries and questions is only one possible interface.

These examples establish product patterns, not proof that an equivalent shopping product will retain users. Original X posts were not consistently accessible; this is a targeted review of accessible first-party material, not an exhaustive review of Greg Isenberg's feed. Product observations below include research supplied by the parent agent.

## Proposed directions for the prototype

1. **Remix a possible version of your life.** Related saves become a visual scene or kit. Users pin elements they want to keep and swap the rest. A budget control changes candidate items while preserving pinned choices. Show linked source material and separate illustrative imagery from actual catalogue products.
2. **Build a shared version.** Two people contribute saves to one project. The app offers combinations that incorporate visible elements from each collection. People can accept, replace or veto components without a questionnaire. Do not infer that silence means agreement.
3. **Try the activity.** An activity collection becomes a small starter experience: a project to attempt, materials to arrange and an explicitly optional list of missing items. Existing possessions can reduce the list. The output should help someone try a hobby before committing to expensive purchases.
4. **Return to a personal drop.** A small set of new compositions draws on recent saves and previous choices. Each is editable and leads to a real next step. Avoid endless generated content that merely creates another backlog.

For the hackathon, the strongest bounded test is one visual project that changes immediately when an item is swapped or a constraint is changed. The AI contribution is the synthesis across source material and consistent adaptation of the whole result. Measure whether users can reach a useful version faster and with less typing, and whether they want to revisit it.
