# Reel-to-purchase interaction concept

A standalone interactive exploration from Chime’s product research. Open `mynah-reel-to-purchase.html` in a modern browser; no dependencies or backend are required.

The six-step example moves from saving an illustrative outfit reel through budget/shape/colour/care trade-offs to simulated checkout. All products, prices and checkout results are fictional and labelled in the interface. It does not capture social media, call a model, use a real catalogue or place an order. It is not wired into the Chrome extension, web app or iOS app.

This preserves an explored design, not a final product direction. Later discussion emphasised synthesising multiple saves into an editable proposal rather than only finding a similar item. See [the research archive](../../docs/research/README.md) for context.

The original concept was exercised in the browser in its research thread, including conflicting budget/style requirements and the simulated checkout. Publication preserves that HTML unchanged; its inline JavaScript syntax was checked again.
